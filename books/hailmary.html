"Project Hail Mary", by Andy Weir

Synopsis

Ryland Grace wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of who he is or why he's there — just two dead crewmates and a growing sense that something has gone terribly wrong. As his memories slowly return, so does the weight of what's at stake: the survival of every living thing on Earth. Alone, millions of miles from home, he has to solve the unsolvable. Or almost alone.
