Proyect #1

"We'll Prescribe You A Cat", by Ishida Syou

Synopsis

The Kokoro Clinic for the Soul is back — a mysterious place with no fixed address that can always be found by those who truly need it. Its prescription? A cat. In this charming sequel, a new cast of feline healers arrives, each with their own personality, ready to mend the emotional wounds of whoever walks through the door.
