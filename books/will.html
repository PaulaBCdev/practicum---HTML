Proyect #1

"The Will of the Many", by James Islington

Synopsis

Vis Telimus is a lie. Smuggled into the Hierarchy's most prestigious academy under a false identity, he has one real mission: find answers, solve a murder, and uncover secrets that could bring the Republic crashing down — all while pretending to be exactly the kind of obedient student the empire wants to create. One wrong move and he's dead. But giving up was never an option for someone who watched his family get executed by the very people he now has to impress.
