Proyect #1

"The Peninsula of Empty Houses", by David Uclés

Synopsis

A family falls apart. A people loses its humanity. A land tears itself to pieces. This is the Spanish Civil War told through the lives of the forgotten — a soldier who cuts open his own skin to let the ash out, a teacher who trains his students to play dead, a photographer who steps on a mine and doesn't lift his foot for forty years. Magic and brutality share the same breath here, as an olive farming family's fate becomes tangled with those of Lorca, Hemingway, Picasso and Orwell. A sprawling, poetic, and deeply strange portrait of a country in its final agony.
