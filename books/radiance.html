"Words of Radiance", by Brandon Sanderson

Synopsis

Kaladin, now commanding the royal bodyguards, must protect the king while quietly mastering his growing powers. Across Roshar, a deadly assassin is targeting rulers one by one — and the people Kaladin protects are next on the list. Meanwhile, Shallan races to uncover secrets that could prevent an ancient, world-ending catastrophe, and deep in the Shattered Plains, the Parshendi make a desperate choice that could change everything.
