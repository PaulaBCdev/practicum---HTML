Proyect #1

"The Silent Patient", by Alex Michaelides

Synopsis

Alicia Berenson had everything — a successful career, a beautiful home, a loving husband. Then one night she shot him five times and never said another word. No explanation, no trial testimony, nothing. Years later, psychotherapist Theo Faber becomes obsessed with cracking her silence. But the deeper he digs into Alicia's story, the more his own unravels.
